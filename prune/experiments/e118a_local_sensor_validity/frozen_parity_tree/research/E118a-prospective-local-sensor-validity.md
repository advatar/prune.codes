# E118a — Prospective local sensor validity on Prune

Status: **prospectively frozen before E118a outcome generation**

The concrete contract is
`experiments/configs/e118a_local_sensor_validity.json`; its complete score-blind
candidate plan is `experiments/configs/e118a_candidate_plan.json`. Both must be
committed before any E118a pack outcome is generated or inspected.

## Why this experiment exists

E118 falsified the end-to-end transfer claim that a sparse causal representation learned on the synthetic substrate would improve Prune strategy search on held-out SWE-bench repositories. The causal arm degraded more than the raw arm even though probe overhead was modest. E118 therefore does not justify another closed-loop search. It motivates a cheaper diagnostic: determine whether the transferred causal measurement contains any prospective, local ranking information among sibling mutations on the target substrate.

E118a deliberately removes trajectory effects. It does **not** ask whether a mutation chosen now leads to a better lineage several generations later. It asks whether, from one frozen parent and one frozen candidate set, the sensor ranks actual Stage-A effects at all.

## Hypotheses

For candidate mutation `j` from parent `p` in repository `r`, let:

- `R_rpj` be the frozen raw predictor score;
- `C_rpj` be the frozen transferred E118 sparse-causal score;
- `G_rpj` be the measured actual Stage-A gain.

E118a separates two claims:

1. **Local signal**: within a parent, `C` positively ranks `G`.
2. **Incremental local signal**: within a parent, `C` contains positive rank information about `G` conditional on `R`.

The second claim is the load-bearing gate. Correlation that merely duplicates the raw predictor is not enough to justify a target-calibration experiment.

## Prospective freeze requirements

Before any candidate's `actual_gain` is inspected, commit a concrete config derived from `experiments/configs/e118a_local_sensor_validity.template.json` with:

- `protocol_frozen: true`;
- the exact E118 result/artifact digest in `source_e118_digest`;
- an immutable identifier/hash for the transferred E118 sensor in `transferred_sensor_id`;
- an immutable identifier/hash for the Stage-A utility computation in `stage_a_metric_id`;
- the exact evaluation repository IDs;
- all thresholds and the permutation seed.

The actual-gain repository set is the six frozen E118 train repositories. The
transferred sensor is not a fitted model: it is E118's candidate-minus-parent
Stage-A gain measured on one rotating, hash-defined E118 probe shard. The raw
comparator is the unchanged ridge model fitted by E118 on those six train
repositories. E118a performs no fitting or target calibration.

For every panel, `causal_score` uses only the E118 probe rows assigned to its
frozen shard and `actual_gain` uses all frozen E118 train rows for the panel's
target repository. These instance sets and repository splits are disjoint. The
four E118 evaluation repositories are excluded from collection, fitting,
threshold selection, and protocol changes.

The 12 panels cycle prospectively over the E118 base, raw-final, and
causal-final parent strategies. Each panel contains all 12 score-blind mutation
opportunities generated by the exact E118 `mutation_spec` and `apply_mutation`
implementation under seed `11802037`. The config binds the complete candidate
plan and a normalized identity manifest by SHA-256.

The analyzer fails closed if the config is not marked frozen or if the input provenance identifiers differ from the frozen config.

## Eligibility

The frozen sample gates are:

- at least 8 distinct, non-no-op siblings per eligible parent;
- at least 12 eligible parents;
- all 6 frozen actual-gain repositories represented.

The exact E118 mutation operator can emit duplicate configs and boundary-clamped
no-ops. All 144 generated rows remain in the evidence, but exact duplicate
configs after their first occurrence and candidates equal to the parent are
deterministically excluded from the statistics. A pre-outcome inspection of the
frozen candidate plan found 20 duplicate-config rows and 3 no-op rows, leaving
at least 8 effective siblings in every panel. This prevents duplicate pack
outcomes from inflating the permutation sample.

If these minima are not met, verdict is `INCONCLUSIVE_INSUFFICIENT_ELIGIBLE_PARENTS`, never PASS or FAIL.

The sample-size thresholds may not be relaxed after evaluation.

## Primary statistics

All ranks are computed within parent with average ranks for ties, then centered within parent before pooling.

The analyzer reports:

- pooled within-parent Spearman-style rank correlation for raw vs actual gain;
- pooled within-parent rank correlation for causal vs actual gain;
- partial rank correlation `corr(C, G | R)`;
- mean top-1 and top-4 selection regret for raw and causal selectors;
- exact expected random top-k regret for each parent, then averaged;
- parent-level correlations/regrets for heterogeneity inspection.

Significance is assessed with a one-sided permutation test that shuffles causal scores **only among siblings of the same parent**. This preserves parent/repository structure and the observed raw/actual relationship.

## Frozen gate

With default `alpha = 0.05`, **local signal** passes iff all are true:

1. pooled within-parent causal rank correlation is positive;
2. its one-sided within-parent permutation p-value is `<= alpha`;
3. causal top-1 regret is lower than the exact random-selection expectation.

**Incremental signal** passes iff all are true:

1. pooled partial rank correlation `corr(C, G | R)` is positive;
2. its one-sided within-parent permutation p-value is `<= alpha`.

E118a returns `PASS_PROCEED_TO_E118B` **only if both gates pass**.

Otherwise it returns `FAIL_NO_PROSPECTIVE_INCREMENTAL_LOCAL_VALIDITY` and E118b must not be run under this protocol.

Top-4 regret is diagnostic rather than a third significance gate because a parent with four siblings makes top-4 regret identically zero. It becomes increasingly informative when larger sibling sets are available.

## Interpretation boundary

PASS means only that the frozen transferred sensor contains prospective local ranking information on Prune, including information not reducible to the frozen raw ranker. It does not establish:

- closed-loop improvement;
- multi-generation compounding;
- held-out SWE-bench repository transfer;
- benefit from a target-calibrated sensor;
- benefit for an actual coding agent.

Those remain E118b, E119, and E120 respectively.

FAIL is scientifically useful: it localizes the E118 failure before trajectory interaction. The next work should then target the mutation/search substrate or redesign how improvement coordinates are measured, rather than tuning the closed loop.

The collection tool must run under E118's pinned virtual environment. It first
verifies the E118 result, runner, function, model, metric, dataset, manifest,
split, binary, and candidate-plan digests. A private copy-on-write cache is used
so the frozen E118 cache and result remain untouched. The outcome command also
requires a clean Parity worktree with the candidate plan already tracked.

After the freeze commit, collect the sibling log and analyze it:

```bash
python -m paritylab.e118a_local_sensor_validity \
  --config experiments/configs/e118a_local_sensor_validity.json \
  --input path/to/e118a_mutations.json \
  --artifact experiments/e118a_local_sensor_validity.json \
  --code-revision "$(git rev-parse HEAD)"
```

Independent verification uses
`tools/validate_e118a_local_sensor_validity.py`, which imports neither the
production analyzer nor its classifier and reconstructs all decision-bearing
statistics. Its permutations shuffle only inside each
`(repository, parent_id)` group.

A FAIL exits non-zero. INCONCLUSIVE exits zero but does not license E118b.

## Frozen result

The protocol and all 144 candidate identities were committed and pushed at
`aef5613e32cf0d8436d03d8557532a8bfe79c0cf` before the first E118a pack
measurement. The collection recorded 12 eligible parents across all six target
repositories. After deterministic removal of 20 repeated configs and three
boundary no-ops, 121 distinct mutations entered the statistics.

The canonical verdict is
`FAIL_NO_PROSPECTIVE_INCREMENTAL_LOCAL_VALIDITY`:

- pooled within-parent causal/gain rank correlation: `0.09740982292363289`;
- one-sided within-parent permutation p-value: `0.16128387161283872`;
- partial causal/gain rank correlation given raw: `0.10793451225738734`;
- one-sided partial-correlation permutation p-value: `0.138986101389861`;
- mean top-1 regret: causal `0.023318223683796307`, raw
  `0.0034664952666666814`, exact random expectation `0.019499789264260704`.

The independent verifier reproduced every value and the verdict. It also
confirmed that permutation was strictly within parent groups and that the
collection accessed only E118 train/probe rows, with zero historical held-out
evaluation accesses.

This falsifies the frozen transferred one-shard sensor as a source of
prospective incremental local ranking validity on this substrate. It does not
show that every substrate-specific sensor must fail, and it does not revise the
historical E118 result. Under the frozen rule, E118b is not licensed and the
sensor must not be retuned post hoc on these outcomes.
